The four ffishAsia scans are of pinned specimens: `prototype/tools/butterflies/butterfly_prep.py` lightened them (7 000 triangles,
colour and relief baked into 1024 px textures), turned them head +X, and gave them a hinge (two wing bones) and a wingbeat clip.

The CC BY-SA models are unchanged (renamed files; their textures are imported at 512 px by the engine).
