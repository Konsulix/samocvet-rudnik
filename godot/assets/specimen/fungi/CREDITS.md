# Mushrooms

Scans and models of real mushrooms (SpecFungi.KINDS). Every one was changed for the game by
`prototype/tools/mushrooms/shroom_prep.py`: stood on its foot one unit tall, made light (1 800–6 000 triangles), its
colour and relief baked into two 512 px textures (`<name>_d.png`, `<name>_n.png`). The originals are not in the repository.
